EmbeddingGemma 2

A 271M-parameter text encoder: a Gemma 4 style layer stack that reads the whole text in both directions and ends in one 768-dimensional vector per text. There is no decoding and no KV cache; every text is a single forward pass, and several texts share one batch. Each step of the GGUF dataflow is drawn with the kernel the engine launches for it. Click any box for details.

Whole model

24 layers in 4 groups of five local layers (each token sees ±512 positions of its own text) and one global layer (the whole text). Before the layers, every token gets 24 extra 512-value inputs, one per layer. Click a layer box to show it below. The first step of every layer, ×3 q, k, v, has its own page comparing the three engines.

Shapes are rows × cols, that is output × input, the way the kernels index them; the GGUF header lists them the other way round (512 × 1024 for attn_q.weight of a local layer). The engine loads the BF16 or the Q8_0 file and keeps every matrix as Q8_0 on the GPU (the Q8_0 file stores per_layer_model_proj in BF16; it is quantized at load).
