MiniMax-H3 (text to video with sound)

MiniMax-H3 turns a prompt into a short video with a matching stereo soundtrack, both generated together. It is four networks: a 50-layer cut of the Qwen3-VL-32B language model encodes the prompt; a 50-block diffusion transformer denoises video and audio latents in one sequence with the prompt, every block's norms and residual adds modulated per token kind (AdaLN) by vectors computed from the timestep; a 36-layer ViT decodes the video latents into frames; BigVGAN decodes the audio latents into 32 kHz samples. The engine runs them one after the other, each with the whole 12 GB GPU, with 16 kernels written for it. The quantized matrices (Q4_K, Q6_K) are expanded to f16 slice by slice (dequant_q4k) right before one tensor-core GEMM (gemm_f16), whose epilogue also applies AdaLN's gate. What is new in minimaxh3.ptx

Everything: hashing every .entry with its comments stripped against the nine other files finds no shared kernel. Four names exist elsewhere with different code (gemm_f16, layernorm, qk_norm_rope, swiglu); gemm_f16's main loop is SAM 3's, its epilogue new.

The 16 kernels

Four networks, one GPU

The files hold 35 GB, the GPU 12. The engine never holds two networks at once: each is loaded into one arena, run, and the arena is reset. The diffusion transformer's 50 blocks (10.8 GB of Q4_K) all fit next to their activations, so a sampling step reads no weights over PCIe; if they don't (a smaller GPU, or MINIMAXH3_STREAM), the extra blocks stream through two slots from pinned host memory while the previous block computes, bit-identical. The text encoder's 15.5 GB cannot fit: 36 layers are copied in and 14 pass through two slots, straight from the mapped file, one layer ahead of the compute.

One sequence, three kinds of tokens

The diffusion transformer attends over [prompt | audio left | audio right | video patches] at once, with a 3-D RoPE whose time axis lines the audio frames up with the video frames. Each token's modulation is picked by a one-byte segment id (timestep row × 3 + modality), read by rms_mod for the norms and by the GEMM epilogue for the gates, so no kernel ever splits the sequence. The audio has its own noise schedule (shift 3 against the video's 12) inside the same sampler: the network sees the audio latents rescaled to their own sigma and returns a velocity converted back.

Same noise as the reference

stable-diffusion.cpp draws its noise with a Philox counter RNG and a Box–Muller step that mixes float and double C math; the engine reproduces it bit for bit (all 46,592 values for seed 42), so a seed gives the same starting point in both.

Tensors of one layer of each network, plus the shared ones

Names as in the GGUF and safetensors files. Shapes are rows × cols, output × input. Q4_K and Q6_K stay in their blocks on the GPU and are expanded per slice; f16 matrices are used as stored; norms, biases and the small heads are f32.

Measured

RTX 4070 SUPER, 256 × 256, 22 frames (0.9 s at 24 fps). The reference is stable-diffusion.cpp (CUDA backend, the same four files).
