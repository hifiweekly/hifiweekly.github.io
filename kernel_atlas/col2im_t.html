MiniMax-H3 · minimaxh3.ptx · BigVGAN's upsampling: a GEMM, then a gather per output sample

Inside col2im_t

BigVGAN grows 37 latent frames into 29,600 samples through 7 transposed convolutions (strides 5, 5, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2). The engine has no convolution kernel: every convolution is a gemm_f16 on a rearranged matrix. A forward convolution reads im2col1d's matrix, each row the K · Cin inputs under the kernel at one output position. A transposed convolution goes the other way. The GEMM multiplies each input sample's Cin channels by the weights to get Cout · K contributions, z[s][co · K + k], one per output channel and tap, and this kernel adds them where they land: output t collects every (s, k) with s · r + k − pad = t, plus the bias. Each output is computed by one thread that gathers, so no atomics are needed.

y[t][co] = b[co] + Σ(s, k): s·r + k − pad = t z[s][co·K + k],   z = x · Wᵀ (W as [co·K + k][ci])   // Lout = (Lin − 1)·r + K − 2·pad = r·Lin

Why it is built this way

    

      

        
Gather, not scatter

        
The natural way to write a transposed convolution is a scatter: each input sample adds its K taps into the output, and neighbouring inputs (K > r) add into the same outputs, which on a GPU means atomics or a careful schedule. Turned around, every output knows its sources: k runs over the taps with k ≡ t + pad (mod r), and s = (t + pad − k) / r. A thread per (t, co) loops over k, keeps the ones that divide evenly and land inside the input, and writes its sum once.

        
All the arithmetic is in the GEMM

        
For the first stage the GEMM is 37 × 4,608 × 1,024 (Cout · K = 512 · 9 by Cin) and this kernel then adds at most 2 values per output: the expensive part runs on tensor cores at the input's length, before the signal has grown. The weights are transposed once at load, from the file's [Cin][Cout][K] to the GEMM's [Cout · K][Cin] rows, and converted to f16.

      

      

        
Small channel counts, padded

        
The GEMM needs N a multiple of 128 and K a multiple of 32. From stage 6 on Cout · K drops to 64 and 32 (16 · 4, 8 · 4), and the last stage's input has 16 channels: the host pads the weight matrices with zero rows and columns, and the activations live in rows of max(C, 128) floats. The extra zeros cost little: those stages are long (7,400 to 29,600 samples) but narrow.

        
im2col1d with dilation

        
The residual stacks use dilations 1, 3 and 5 with kernels 3, 7 and 11, "same" padding (K·d − d)/2: row t of the matrix holds x[t − pad + k·d][ci] for each (ci, k), zeros outside the signal and in the padding columns up to a multiple of 32. It converts to f16 on the way, so the matrix is the GEMM's A operand as it is. 128 im2col1d and 128 GEMMs per channel, 7 col2im_t.
