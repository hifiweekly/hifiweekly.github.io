gemma3.ptx - hand-written PTX kernels for Gemma 3 inference. The host (gemma3.c) runs one decoder layer as (T tokens at a time): xn = rmsnorm(x) * attn_norm (from the previous add_rmsnorm) q, k, v = Wq . xn, Wk . xn, Wv . xn mv_q8 / gemm_q8 q, k = rmsnorm(q|k head) * q|k_norm, qk_norm_rope (also writes k, v then RoPE to the f16 KV cache) att = softmax(q . K^T / 16) . V attention (sliding window of 1024 on 5 of every 6 layers) y = Wo . att mv_q8 / gemm_q8 x += rmsnorm(y) * post_attn_norm; add_rmsnorm xn = rmsnorm(x) * ffn_norm g|u = [Wgate; Wup] . xn quant_q8 + mv_q5 / gemm_q5 h = gelu(g) * u geglu y = Wdown . h quant_q8 + mv_q5 / gemm_q5 x += rmsnorm(y) * post_ffw_norm; add_rmsnorm xn = rmsnorm(x) * (next layer's attn_norm, or output_norm) and finally logits = Wemb . xn (the tied embedding matrix), next = argmax. Weight formats (row-major, a row = one output feature): Q8: int8 values q[rows][cols] plus one f16 scale per 32 values d[rows][cols/32]; w = q * d. Attention and the output head; they multiply f32 activations directly. Q5: per 32 values one f16 scale d[rows][cols/32], the low 4 bits of the values in qs[rows][cols/2] (in a block, byte j holds value j in its low nibble and value j+16 in its high nibble) and their 5th bits in qh[rows][cols/32] (bit j = value j); w = (q - 16) * d. The feed-forward matrices. Their activations are quantized to Q8 in blocks of 32 (int8 + f32 scale), as llama.cpp does, so the inner products are dp4a integer dot products. These are ggml's Q8_0 and Q5_0 blocks with the fields in separate arrays, so every load is aligned.
